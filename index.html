One Unified University Event Platform

Engineered for hybrid academic excellence

Combining the cleanest workflows of EventsAir, Whova, EasyChair, and Cvent — built specifically for Wits School of Therapeutic Sciences with Supabase Auth, Storage, and Realtime.

01 · PLAN & REVIEW

Academic Submissions & Peer Review

Structured multi-track call for abstracts, blind rubric scoring, conflict-of-interest checks, and author revisions.

  • Track-routed PDF, slide deck & game build uploads
  • 4-criterion scientific committee scorecard
  • 1-click promotion of accepted talks to agenda
02 · ENGAGE

App-Like Experience on Every Device

Installable Progressive Web App with automatic mobile/desktop layout switching — no app store download barrier.

  • Personal QR Check-In Pass & `.ICS` calendar sync
  • Inter-university networking & direct messaging
  • Campus + Online Amazing Race & QR Scavenger Hunt
03 · DELIVER

Seamless On-Ground & Online Hybrid

Equitable participation for delegates in the Parktown auditorium and colleagues joining from anywhere in the world.

  • Zoom & Microsoft Teams multi-stage integration
  • Realtime upvotable Q&A & live audience polls
  • Instant slide decks, rulebooks & session docs
04 · PARTNER & SCALE

Sponsors, Exhibitors & New Events

Reusable multi-event architecture so every STHS department can launch branded sub-sites in minutes.

  • Interactive 3D Sponsorship Prospectus Flipbook
  • Exhibitor lead capture & partner ROI metrics
  • Clean `/sthsevent/<event-slug>` routing
